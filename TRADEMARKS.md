# tsudev trademark policy

_Tiếng Việt ở cuối trang._

The source code of tsudev projects is open source (see each repository's `LICENSE`). **The brand is not.** The names
**tsudev**, **Lotus Share**, **cwico**, **tsudev SWICO**, **tsudev-files**, **tsudev-contact**,
**tsudev-trafficmonitor**, the tsudev owl logo and every product logo are trademarks of tsudev. Open-source licenses
cover copyright, not trademarks (Apache-2.0 section 6 says so explicitly).

## You may, without asking

- Say that your software is "based on tsudev", "a fork of Lotus Share", or "compatible with tsudev" - as plain text.
- Link to tsudev.com and use the unmodified logo in articles, reviews and tutorials that talk about tsudev.
- Package unmodified official releases for a distribution under the original name.

## You may not, without written permission

- Ship a modified build, fork or service under a tsudev name or logo, or a confusingly similar one.
- Remove or alter copyright notices, `NOTICE` files or "made by tsudev" attributions (this also breaks the license).
- Suggest that tsudev endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with your product.
- Use tsudev marks in domain names, app store listings or ads for anything that is not an official tsudev release.

A fork must use its own name and logo and keep the original `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files.

Permission requests: open an issue in [tsudev-org/.github](https://github.com/tsudev-org/.github/issues).
